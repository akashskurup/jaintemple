A guide to what you are looking at

Four things worth finding in the photographs

The dharmachakra

Above the gate, between two golden lions — the wheel of dharma, the emblem you will find on the pediment of almost every Jain temple built in this style.

The tortoise

Munisuvrat Swami’s lanchhan is cut into the base of the pedestal, among the lions and attendant figures below the idol. It is small, and it is the sign that identifies which Jina this is.

Chhatras and inlay

The hanging three-tier chhatras painted on the gabhara wall, and the coloured stone inlay — swastik, kalash, lotus, ashtamangal — set into the white marble around them.

The pratishtha plaques

Three marble panels below the idols, written in Gujarati, naming the families from Kosamba, Vadodara and Dahegam who installed each Bhagwan.
