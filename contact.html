Quick answers

Things people usually ring to ask

Very welcome. Telephone a day or two ahead so the Sangh knows to expect you and can fit the darshan around the puja schedule. Groups on tirth-yatra passing through Vadodara often stop here.

Ring the Sangh with the date or tithi you have in mind, and the name it should be announced in. Parva dates are taken early, so ask well ahead. The seva page lists everything that is commonly sponsored.

There is a morning and an evening window, with the sanctum resting through the early afternoon — the darshan page lays out the whole day. Parva days run to a different schedule, so a quick call is worth it.

The pathshala meets in the hall behind the derasar. Ask for the current days and timings, and for the vacation shibir schedule when it is announced.

The Jain Anukampa Group, Karelibaug handles medical and medicine assistance and runs health camps from the Sangh’s premises. Mention anukampa when you call, and it will be passed to the right people.

Send them on WhatsApp. Photographs of the mahotsavs, the angi rachnas and the pathshala are always welcome, as are the current year’s parva dates.
