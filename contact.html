Help us get it right

Corrections welcome — and wanted

This website was assembled from what the derasar itself publishes: its Google Business Profile, the photographs posted there, and the boards and inscriptions visible in those photographs. That gets the address, the phone number, the deities and the architecture right. It leaves several things blank.

If you are connected with the Sangh, these are the details that would make the site properly complete — and each is a two-minute message:

The actual darshan and aarti timings

The single most useful correction. The schedule currently shown is the customary Shwetambar routine, clearly marked as indicative, because no hours are published anywhere. Real times — including the summer and winter variation — would replace it immediately.

The trust’s name and office-bearers

The registered name of the trust, and the trustees or karyakars who should be listed as points of contact for puja bookings and seva.

The year of pratishtha and the temple’s history

The pratishtha date is recorded on a marble plaque above the gabhara but is not legible in the public photographs. The account of how the derasar came to be built in Karelibaug — the acharya whose upadesh prompted it, the families who carried it — belongs on the About page.

This year’s parva dates and mahotsav programme

The tithis are on the Parva page already. The English dates for the current year, and the Sangh’s own programme for Paryushan, Posh Dashami and Mahavir Janma Kalyanak, would turn it into a working calendar.

Photographs, and photograph credits

Better and more recent photographs are very welcome — of the mahotsavs, the pathshala, the angi rachnas. So are corrections to the captions, or credit lines for any image whose photographer we have not named.

Pathshala and anukampa details

Pathshala days and timings, the shibir schedule, and how the Jain Anukampa Group, Karelibaug prefers to be contacted for medical assistance.
