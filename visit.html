Get directions

Read this before you set out — the entrance. Several visitors have noted on Google that the gate facing the main road is often kept closed, and that entry is instead from the side street, up about eleven steps to the temple floor.

There is no ramp or lift. If anyone in your group uses a wheelchair or cannot manage stairs, please ring 099674 76524 in advance — the Sangh will tell you which approach is open on the day and whether help can be arranged.

Getting here

From wherever you are starting

By auto or taxi

The simplest way. Ask for Karelibaug, Arya Kanya Vidyalaya — every auto driver in Vadodara knows the school, and the derasar is directly opposite it on Pratapkunj Society Road. Autos wait on the lane outside.

Landmark to say aloud: “Arya Kanya Vidyalaya samé, Jain derasar.”

From Vadodara Junction

The railway station is a short ride away — usually ten to fifteen minutes by auto, longer in the evening peak. Head towards Karelibaug and then to Arya Kanya Vidyalaya.

City buses also serve Karelibaug; ask for the stop nearest Arya Kanya Vidyalaya.

From Vadodara Airport

Vadodara (Harni) airport lies on the same side of the city as Karelibaug, so the drive is a short one — typically well under half an hour outside peak traffic.

Use the plus code 858X+CV if your driver’s app cannot find the name.

Parking

There is no dedicated car park. Two-wheelers and autos stand along Pratapkunj Society Road; a “No Parking” board is posted on part of the compound wall, so read the signage before leaving a car.

On mahotsav evenings the lane fills quickly — come early or park further out and walk.

Practical notes

Small things that help

  • Footwear off at the gate. Racks are provided. Carry a cloth bag if you would rather keep your shoes with you.
  • Leave leather in the vehicle. Belt, wallet, watch strap, handbag — this is the one rule worth checking twice before you walk in.
  • Morning is cooler. Vadodara summers are severe and the marble floor gets hot; the early slot is far more comfortable.
  • Call ahead on parva days. Timings shift completely during Paryushan, Posh Dashami and Mahavir Janma Kalyanak.
  • Read the compound wall. The current mahotsav banners, programme and labharthi lists are posted right there on the railings.
  • Keep it quiet. This is a residential society street, and the calm is what people come for.

Shankheshwar & the great tirths

Shankheshwar, Shatrunjay (Palitana) and Girnar are all reachable from Vadodara on a yatra of a few days — and all three appear on the tirth-patas hanging in this derasar’s hall.

Ratings as listed on Google. Please check each temple’s own timings before visiting.

One call settles everything

Timings, the open gate, whether a puja is on, whether a mahotsav is running — the Sangh will tell you in half a minute.
