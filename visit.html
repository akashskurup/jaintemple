Getting here

From wherever you are starting

By auto or taxi

The simplest way. Ask for Karelibaug, Arya Kanya Vidyalaya — every auto driver in Vadodara knows the school, and the derasar stands directly opposite it on Pratapkunj Society Road. Autos wait on the lane outside.

Landmark to say aloud: “Arya Kanya Vidyalaya samé, Jain derasar.”

From Vadodara Junction

A short ride — usually ten to fifteen minutes by auto, longer at the evening peak. Head for Karelibaug, then Arya Kanya Vidyalaya. City buses serve Karelibaug too.

From the airport

Vadodara (Harni) airport is on the same side of the city as Karelibaug, so the drive is short — typically well under half an hour outside peak traffic.

If the app cannot find the name

Give your driver the plus code 858X+CV, or the coordinates 22.3161, 73.1997. Both drop the pin exactly at the compound gate.

Practical notes

Small things that help

  • Footwear off at the gate. Racks are provided; carry a cloth bag if you would rather keep your shoes with you.
  • Leave leather in the vehicle. Belt, wallet, watch strap, handbag — the one thing worth checking twice before you walk in.
  • Morning is cooler. Vadodara summers are severe and the marble floor warms up; the early slot is far more comfortable.
  • Ring ahead on parva days — timings shift completely during Paryushan, Posh Dashami and Mahavir Janma Kalyanak.
  • Read the compound wall. The current mahotsav banners, programme and labharthi lists are posted right there on the railings.
  • Two-wheelers and autos stand along Pratapkunj Society Road; on mahotsav evenings the lane fills early, so come in good time.
  • Keep it quiet. This is a residential society street, and the calm is what people come for.
Making a round of it

Other derasars nearby, and the great tirths

Vadodara has a dense cluster of derasars and mandirs, several of them a short ride from Karelibaug — and the tirths on this derasar’s own hall walls are all reachable from the city.

Around Karelibaug

Both Shwetambar and Digambar temples sit within the surrounding wards, along with the Digambar Jain Sangh premises in Karelibaug itself. A morning is enough to walk several of them.

Shankheshwar

Shri Shankheshwar Parshwanath — one of the four invocations on the mantra plaque here, and a day trip north from Vadodara.

Shatrunjay & Girnar

Palitana’s hill of temples and the climb at Junagadh — both painted on the tirth-patas in the hall behind the derasar, and both a few days’ yatra from here.

One call settles everything

Timings, whether a puja is on, whether a mahotsav is running — the Sangh will tell you in half a minute.
